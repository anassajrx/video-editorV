Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

video-platform is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.

Google data it accesses

  • Google Drive: it reads the video files the owner selects and writes the edited results into a video-editor folder in the owner's own Drive.
  • Gmail (send only): it sends the owner a notification email when a video is ready. It does not read email.

How the data is used and stored

Videos are processed only to produce the owner's edited video. Temporary copies exist only on the processing machines during a job and are deleted when the job ends. Results stay in the owner's Google Drive. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
